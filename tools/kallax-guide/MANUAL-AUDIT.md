## Exact body sequence: AA-2703308-1

The manual assembles the carcass with cube openings facing upward. The final floor-standing orientation is introduced after step 8. Board names below refer to their eventual function; the manual itself uses pictures.

| Manual step | PDF page | Action and new hardware |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 12 | Join one long outer panel and one shorter outer side at a right angle. 2 long screws, hex key. |
| 2 | 13 | Fit the FIRST SHORT divider to the midpoint of the first outer panel using 2 dowels. Warning inset explicitly rejects using the long divider here. Hammer shown tapping the dowels. |
| 3 | 14 | Fit the full-span thin middle divider, meeting the first short divider and joining the existing outer side. 2 dowels at the outer-side connection. |
| 4 | 15 | Insert 2 dowels through the two centre-face holes of the long middle divider, into the first short divider behind it. |
| 5 | 16 | Fit the second short divider to the exposed ends of those same centre dowels. |
| 6 | 17 | Insert 4 dowels into the exposed free edges: 2 in the long middle divider and 2 in the second short divider. |
| 7 | 18 | Fit the second shorter outer side to the long divider. Orientation detail shows the small inner-face holes near its end. |
| 8 | 19 | Fit the final long outer panel, then secure all remaining corners with 6 long screws. Four are at the new outer panel, two complete the previously fitted side's connection to the first outer panel. |

Dowel budget: 2 + 2 + 2 + 4 = 10. The two central dowels are shared through the middle divider; do not create four at that crossing. Screw budget: 2 + 6 = 8. No additional dowels for step 7 or 8.

## Alternative 14-dowel manual differences

AA-1009450-15 body has steps 1-9 rather than 1-8. Step 1 p11: 2 screws. Step 2 p12: first short divider +2 dowels. Step 3 p13: long divider +4 dowels. Step 4 p13: 2 more central dowels. Step 5 p14: second short divider. Step 6 p14: insert6 dowels, including a pair in the first long outer panel's exposed end; step 7 p15: fit the second short outer side; step 8 p15: fit the final long outer panel; step 9 p16: remaining6 screws. The older diagram shows extra dowels at the centre crossing and first outer panel-to-side corner, accounting for the 4-dowel difference.

Floor branch AA-1009450-15: step10 p17 pads; 11 p18 brackets; 12 p19 level/mark; 13 p19 wall screws; 14 p20 lock/tighten/covers. Wall-hung branch: 10 p21 brackets;11 p22 hold level/mark (two people pictured);12 p23 wall screws;13 p24 lock/tighten/covers.

## Dimensions and model limits

Official product page measurements: **765 mm wide, 765 mm high, 390 mm deep**. Marketing size 77 x 77 cm is rounded. Published limits: **13 kg per shelf**, **20 kg max wall cabinet**, **25 kg top load**; wall-hung frame load also depends on wall material. These limits have different meanings and should not be combined.

The manual does **not** dimension panel thicknesses, exact internal cube clearances, hole offsets, dowel protrusion, screw lengths, bracket size, bracket spacing, or material tolerances. Any such 3D modelling numbers are approximate reconstruction and must be described as illustrative. Use source page links to resolve physical hole orientation. Do not present the model as a drilling template.
